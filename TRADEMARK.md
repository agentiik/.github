# Trademark policy

A licence governs copyright, not the name. Apache-2.0 says outright that it "does not
grant permission to use the trade names, trademarks, service marks, or product names of
the Licensor", and the AGPL grants none either. So the right to fork the code and the
right to call the fork Agentiik are separate questions, and this is the answer to the
second.

The name **Agentiik** and the Agentiik mark stay with the project.

## What you may do without asking

- Say that your software uses, runs on, integrates with, or is built for Agentiik.
- Say that your fork is derived from Agentiik, or based on Agentiik.
- Use the name in an article, a talk, a course, a comparison or a review.
- Use the mark unmodified to refer to the project, at a size and a clearance that keep it
  legible, as set out in the design system.

## What you may not do without permission

- Present your fork, distribution, product or hosted service as Agentiik.
- Use the name or the mark as, or inside, your own product name, company name, domain
  name or application name, or in a way that suggests the project endorses or maintains
  what you are offering.
- Alter the mark — recolour it outside the palette, redraw it, set it in another typeface,
  or combine it with another mark — and still call it the Agentiik mark.

## The short version

A fork may say it is derived from Agentiik. It may not present itself as Agentiik.

Ask at <https://github.com/agentiik> if you want to do something this policy does not
cover. Permission is usually a short conversation.
